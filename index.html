Vaporwave: The Nostalgic Aesthetic

Emerging in the early 2010s on platforms like Bandcamp, vaporwave began as an electronic microgenre that has truly formed into an official music genre that is still growing very strong. It helped form Lo-Fi, Chill hop, retrowave, synthwave, and many more. The birth of Vaporwave was started by a niche YouTube video labeled "nobody Here".

Pillars of the Aesthetic

Visual Motifs

Vaporwave visual art combines surreal retro imagery and early digital nostalgia:

  • Classical Roman and Greek bust statues (e.g., Helios)
  • Early Windows 95 and Mac OS desktop interfaces
  • Abandoned 1980s shopping malls and checkerboard floors
  • Aesthetics inspiring the backrooms, dreamcore, and Y2K nostalgia

Musical Production

The sound design emphasizes dreamlike, distorted memory rather than crisp modern production:

  • Slowed tempo and dropped pitch (tape warble effects)
  • Heavy gated reverb imitating empty commercial spaces
  • Loops chopped from forgotten VHS commercials and infomercials

Fun Facts & Honorable Mentions

  1. Vektroid, whose alias was formerly known as Macintosh Plus, is widely known for Floral Shoppe 420.
  2. Artists like Yung Bae, Haircuts For Men, Saint Pespsi (now known as Skylar Spence) are all heavily inspired by Vektroid as well as advant garde artist like Boards of Canada.
  3. Branching out from vaporwave are subgenres such as Mallsoft, Future Funk, and Dreamcore.
  4. Honorable mention goes to my favourite vaporwave artist Nanoshrine, who released works on Bandcamp such as impurity, Segas, funstation, life of the party, and noel.
