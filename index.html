Origins & Cultural Critique

>

Emerging in the early 2010s on platforms like Bandcamp, vaporwave began as an electronic microgenre that has truly formed into an offical music genre that is still growing very strong. It helped form Lo-Fi, Chill hop, retrowave, synthwave, and many more. The birth of Vaporwave was started by a niche youtube video labeled "nobody Here".

Pillars of the Aesthetic

Visual Motifs

Vaporwave visual art combines and it's influences.

  • Classical Roman and Greek bust statues (e.g., Helios)
  • Early Windows 95 and Mac OS desktop interfaces
  • Abandoned 1980s shopping malls and checkerboard floors
  • genre inspired the aesthetic of backrooms, pools, levels, dreamcore, and nostalgia y2k

Musical Production

The sound design emphasizes dreamlike, distorted memory rather than crisp modern production.

  • Slowed tempo and dropped pitch (tape warble effects)
  • Heavy gated reverb imitating empty commercial spaces
  • Loops chopped from forgotten VHS commercials and infomercials
