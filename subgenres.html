Major Electronic Subgenres

Mallsoft

Mallsoft recreates the ambient acoustics of empty 1980s and 1990s shopping malls using heavy reverb, commercial samples, and distant crowd chatter.

Future Funk

An upbeat, dance-oriented beat that speeds up 1980s Japanese City Pop, anime dialogue samples and disco loops with punchy French house, sounding similar to Daft Punk with the japanese spin.

Dreamcore & Slushwave

Downtempo, sample-heavy instrumental loops layered with lush phasing and tape saturation for a hypnotic, late-night atmosphere.

Featured Artist Spotlight: Nanoshrine

Active on Bandcamp, Nanoshrine blends classic late-night lo-fi textures with nostalgic synth melodies across standout releases:

  • impurity — Released in 2015 features hypnotic chopped loops and warm analog hiss.
  • Segas & funstation — Released 2015 features playful nods to 1990s console sound chips and menu screens.
  • life of the party & noel —Released 2016 Atmospheric track about partying with a lover, while Noel released 2018 captures bittersweet seasonal nostalgia.
